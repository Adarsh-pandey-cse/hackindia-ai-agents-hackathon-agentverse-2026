[Section: Tuition Fee Refund Windows and Eligibility]
1. Scope and Applicability: This policy governs refund calculations for students formally withdrawing from academic programs or requesting cancellation of provisional admission before the close of the academic admission session.
2. 100% Refund Window: A full 100% refund of tuition and academic fees (minus a nominal administrative processing fee) will be granted if formal withdrawal notice is received in writing within 7 days of initial fee payment or at least 15 days before the formally notified date of commencement of classes.
3. Partial Refund Tiers:
   - Withdrawal between 8 and 15 days following payment: 80% of aggregate tuition fees is refunded.
   - Withdrawal between 16 and 30 days following payment: 50% of aggregate tuition fees is refunded.
   - Withdrawal beyond 30 days after payment or after the commencement of second-week classroom lectures: 0% refund (no tuition fees refunded).
4. Application Procedure: All refund claims must be submitted online through the Academic Grievance and Withdrawal Portal alongside banking NEFT/RTGS details and the original digital fee receipt.

[Section: Cancellation Charges, Administrative Deductions, and Timelines]
1. Administrative Processing Deduction: For all eligible refund claims processed within the 100% refund window, an administrative processing fee deduction of Rs 250 is retained to cover verification, banking transaction levies, and record archiving costs.
2. Non-Refundable Fee Components: Admission brochure fees, application processing charges, and student activity union memberships are non-refundable under all circumstances.
3. Hostel Rent and Caution Deposit: The hostel caution deposit and library security deposit are 100% refundable upon completion of the official No-Dues Clearance procedure. Room rent for occupied months is non-refundable; unutilised mess fee balances are calculated pro-rata from the exit date.
4. Disbursement Timelines: Verified refund amounts are disbursed directly to the bank account designated in the withdrawal request within 10 to 14 business days from the date of final departmental clearance.

[Section: Escalation, Disputes, and Appeal Mechanism]
1. Dispute Escalation: If an applicant has questions or disputes regarding deduction amounts or disbursement delays exceeding 14 business days, an inquiry can be escalated to refunds@campus-finance.edu.in.
2. Refund Review Committee: Appeals regarding exceptional medical emergencies or family relocation must be directed to the Appellate Refund Review Committee chaired by the Registrar.
3. Review Timeline: The Review Committee convenes bi-weekly and issues written determinations within 10 business days of appellate docket receipt. Determinations rendered by the Appellate Committee represent the institute's final decision.
