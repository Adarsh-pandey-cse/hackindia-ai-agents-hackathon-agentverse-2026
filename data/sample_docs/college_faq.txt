[Section: Hostel Admissions and Room Allocation]
1. Eligibility: Full-time undergraduate and postgraduate students enrolled in regular degree programs are eligible to apply for on-campus hostel accommodation. First-year outstation students receive priority during the monsoon semester intake.
2. Room Allocation: Allocation is conducted on a merit-cum-distance basis through the Central Housing Portal. Students must confirm their allotment within 5 working days of the offer letter by paying the room acceptance deposit.
3. Check-in Requirements: At the time of check-in, students must present their original institute ID card, medical fitness certificate, and a signed code of conduct agreement.
4. Curfew and Entry Hours: The main hostel gates close at 10:30 PM on weekdays and 11:00 PM on weekends. Late entry requires prior written permission from the resident hostel warden.

[Section: Campus Wi-Fi and IT Network Services]
1. Wi-Fi Pass Eligibility: All registered hostel residents and enrolled day scholars are eligible to apply for an active high-speed campus Wi-Fi pass via the Student IT Self-Service Portal.
2. Device Limit: Each student is entitled to register a maximum of two personal devices (for example, one laptop and one mobile smartphone) on the institute 802.1x secure wireless network.
3. Credentials and Security: Network login credentials are tied to the institutional email address and single sign-on (SSO) account. Sharing credentials, running unauthorised hotspot servers, or operating peer-to-peer torrent clients is strictly prohibited and results in immediate 14-day network suspension.
4. IT Helpdesk Support: Technical issues regarding network connectivity or device authentication can be reported via email to helpdesk@campus-it.edu.in or by visiting Room 104 in the Computer Centre between 9:00 AM and 5:00 PM on working days.

[Section: Library Rules, Access, and Overdue Penalties]
1. Working Hours: The Central Library remains open from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM on Mondays through Saturdays, and from 10:00 AM to 6:00 PM on Sundays and public holidays. During semester examination periods, reading halls remain accessible 24/7.
2. Borrowing Entitlement: Undergraduate students can borrow up to four books simultaneously for a borrowing period of 14 calendar days. Postgraduate students and research scholars may borrow up to six books for 28 calendar days.
3. Overdue Penalties: Borrowers who fail to return or renew books by the due date are subject to an overdue late return fine of Rs 10 per day per book for the first 7 days of delay, increasing to Rs 20 per day thereafter.
4. Lost or Damaged Books: If a borrowed book is lost or mutilated, the borrower must either replace the volume with an identical or newer edition or pay twice the current market cost of the publication plus a handling surcharge of Rs 100.

[Section: Semester Fee Payment and Financial Deadlines]
1. Payment Channels: Students can pay semester tuition fees, hostel rent, and examination dues electronically via UPI, net banking, or debit/credit cards through the secure Institute ERP Portal. In exceptional circumstances, payment may be made via Demand Draft drawn in favour of 'The Registrar, AgentVerse Institute' payable at New Delhi. Cash payments are not accepted at any campus counter.
2. Standard Due Dates: The scheduled deadline for standard fee payment without penalty is 15 August for the Autumn Semester and 15 January for the Spring Semester.
3. Late Payment Penalties: Any fee payment completed after 15 August is subject to a mandatory late fee fine of Rs 500 up to 31 August. Payments received after 31 August incur an additional cumulative penalty of Rs 100 per day until clearance, subject to approval from the Dean of Academic Affairs.
4. Fee Receipt Verification: Electronic transaction receipts generated by the ERP portal serve as official verification. In case of failed bank debits or disputed transactions, students must raise a ticket with accounts@campus.edu within 3 business days.
